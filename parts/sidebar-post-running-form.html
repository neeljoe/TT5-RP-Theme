Form Checklist

  • Head up, eyes on the horizon
  • Shoulders relaxed, not hunched
  • Arms swing at 90 degrees
  • Midfoot strike, not heel
  • Cadence of 170-180 steps/min
  • Breathing steady and rhythmic

Common Fixes

  • Heel striking — increase cadence, shorter steps
  • Overstriding — land under your center of mass
  • Side stitches — exhale on opposite foot strike
  • Tight shoulders — shake out arms periodically
