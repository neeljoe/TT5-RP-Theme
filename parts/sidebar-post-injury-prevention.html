Prevention Checklist

  • Increase mileage by no more than 10% per week
  • Warm up before every run
  • Include rest days in your schedule
  • Cross-train 1-2 days per week
  • Replace shoes every 300-500 miles
  • Listen to pain — stop if something hurts

When to See a Doctor

Seek medical advice if you experience: sharp or persistent pain, swelling that does not improve with rest, pain that changes your running form, or any symptom that lasts more than two weeks.
