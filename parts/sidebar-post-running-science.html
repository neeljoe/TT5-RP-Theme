Key Terms

  • VO₂ max — max oxygen your body can use during intense exercise
  • Lactate threshold — intensity where lactate accumulates faster than it's cleared
  • Running economy — oxygen cost at a given pace (efficiency)
  • Stride length × cadence = speed
