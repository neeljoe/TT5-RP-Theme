Mental Training Exercises

  • Visualization — picture yourself running strong and finishing well
  • Positive self-talk — replace "I can't" with "I am capable"
  • Process focus — concentrate on form, breathing, and rhythm, not the result
  • Segmenting — break the run into manageable chunks

Race-Day Confidence

  • Review your training log — you did the work
  • Arrive early with a warm-up routine
  • Control what you can: nutrition, sleep, gear
  • Have a mantra ready ("steady and strong")
