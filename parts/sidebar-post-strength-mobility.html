Exercise of the Week

Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift

  1. Stand on one leg, holding a light weight
  2. Hinge at the hip, extending free leg behind
  3. Lower weight toward the floor
  4. Return to start. 3x10 per leg.

Warm-Up Routine

  • Leg swings (forward + lateral) — 30s each
  • Walking lunges with twist — 10 per leg
  • High knees — 30 seconds
  • Butt kicks — 30 seconds
  • Dynamic hamstring stretch — 10 per leg
