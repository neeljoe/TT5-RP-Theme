Pre-Run Fuel

Eat 1-3 hours before running:

  • Banana with peanut butter
  • Toast with honey
  • Oatmeal with berries
  • Rice cakes with avocado

Hydration Guide

  • < 45 min — water is sufficient
  • 45-90 min — water + electrolytes
  • > 90 min — add carbohydrates (gels, sports drink)
  • Drink to thirst — don't overhydrate
